On the last day of my internship at the European Space Operations Centre, I presented Experiment 266 to the OPS-SAT team. The experiment is the core of my [master's thesis](/blog/masters-thesis-ops-sat/).

The presentation covers the memory limits of the SEPP computer, recording directly to the eMMC memory, the waterfall preview, and cutting out only the interesting signals before the download to Earth. At the end, I showed ideas for the future: a rewrite in Python, downsampling during the recording, and signal detection with AI. My conclusion: the main value of the experiment is the operational procedure, more than the technique itself.
