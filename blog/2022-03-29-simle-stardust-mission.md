[SimLE Stardust - misja stratosferyczna z Iławy | 27 Lutego 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vfBzfLm3els)

First stratospheric mission of 2022 organized by SimLE Science Club based at Gdańsk Universityty of Technology.

This mission is a pre-coursor to the new Stratospheric Balloon Programme at the SimLE Science Club: "Stardust NEXT". It's purpose is to summarize the experience and lessons learned from the BEXUS programme, and pass it on to the new generation of students at Gdańsk Tech.
